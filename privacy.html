Privacy policy

Conference Tracker · Last updated 12 November 2026

Conference Tracker helps management researchers find conferences and keep track of deadlines. It is run by Sarnish Kour as an independent, non-commercial project. This page explains what information the app keeps about you and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google. With Google sign-in we receive only your name, email address and profile picture link, nothing else from your Google account.
  • Profile you enter: name, institution, role, country, and your research areas, preferred regions and conference tiers.
  • Your activity in the app: the statuses, notes and reminders you set on conferences.
  • What you send us: feedback, ratings and conference suggestions.
  • Leaderboard: only if you choose to appear, the display name you pick and your points.

How we use it

  • To run the app for you: show matching conferences, keep your lists, and remind you of deadlines.
  • To improve the app and its conference list, using your feedback and suggestions.
  • To show the leaderboard, only for people who have switched it on.

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than running and improving this app.

Who can see it

Your profile, lists and reminders are visible only to you. The app's host can read feedback and suggestions, together with the sender's name, role and institution, so they can respond and improve the app. The host cannot see your conference statuses or notes. Other users see only the leaderboard name you choose, and only if you opt in.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, a database provider, on servers in the Asia-Pacific region, protected by access rules that limit each account to its own records. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, and by Google if you choose Google sign-in.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and preferences at any time in the app.
  • Download your data, or delete it, from Profile → Privacy and your data. Deleting removes your profile, statuses, reminders and history.
  • Turn the leaderboard on or off at any time from the Leaders tab.

Cookies and local storage

The app stores your sign-in session and a few display settings, such as your filters, in your browser. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. For any question or request about your data, contact Sarnish Kour through sarnishkour.github.io.
